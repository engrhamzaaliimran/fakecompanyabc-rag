# FakeCompanyABC Annual Leave Policy

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines annual paid leave, carry-forward rules, and the process for requesting leave at FakeCompanyABC.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement

Annual leave depends on the employee's contractual work location.

### Germany

Full-time employees whose contractual work location is Germany receive **30 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year**.

### Pakistan

Full-time employees whose contractual work location is Pakistan receive **24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year**.

Part-time employees receive annual leave on a pro-rata basis according to their contracted working days.

## 3. Carry-forward

Employees may carry forward a maximum of **5 unused annual-leave days** into the following calendar year.

Carried-forward leave must normally be used by **31 March** of the following year. Any remaining carried-forward leave expires after that date unless HR has approved an exception in writing.

## 4. Leave requests

Leave of **5 consecutive working days or fewer** should normally be requested at least **5 working days in advance**.

Leave of **more than 5 consecutive working days** should normally be requested at least **3 weeks in advance**.

All annual-leave requests require approval from the employee's direct manager.

## 5. Probation

Employees may request annual leave during probation, but approval depends on business needs and manager approval. The annual entitlement itself is not reduced because an employee is on probation.

## 6. Important interpretation note

The annual entitlement is based on the employee's **contractual work location**, not nationality, citizenship, or the country from which the employee happens to work temporarily.

## 7. Examples

- An employee contracted in Germany with 8 unused days at year-end may carry forward only 5 days.
- An employee contracted in Pakistan with 3 unused days may carry all 3 days forward.
- A Germany-based employee temporarily working from Pakistan still follows the Germany annual-leave entitlement unless the employment contract is changed.
