# FakeCompanyABC Expense Reimbursement Policy

## 1. Submission deadline

Employees must normally submit business-expense claims within **30 calendar days** of the expense date.

Claims submitted after 30 days require an explanation and may require additional approval.

## 2. Receipts

A receipt is required for any individual expense above:

- **EUR 20** for expenses paid in euros;
- **PKR 6,000** for expenses paid in Pakistani rupees.

For lower-value expenses without a receipt, the employee must still provide the date, amount, and business purpose.

## 3. Meals during business travel

The standard maximum reimbursable meal expense per person is:

| Location | Maximum per meal |
|---|---:|
| Germany | EUR 35 |
| Belgium | EUR 40 |
| Spain | EUR 35 |
| Pakistan | PKR 6,500 |

Alcoholic beverages are not reimbursable under the standard meal allowance.

## 4. Hotels

Hotel room-rate limits are **not defined by this policy**.

For hotel limits, employees must use the current **Business Travel Policy, HR-TRV-0173**.

This is intentional: the expense policy defines how claims are submitted, while the travel policy defines allowable hotel rates.

## 5. Ground transportation

Reasonable public-transport expenses are reimbursable for approved business travel.

Taxi or ride-hailing expenses are reimbursable when public transport is impractical, unavailable, or would create an unreasonable delay. The business reason should be included in the expense claim.

## 6. Personal purchases

Personal purchases are not reimbursable.

If a receipt contains both business and personal items, only the business portion may be claimed.

## 7. Reimbursement timing

Approved expenses are normally reimbursed with the next available payroll cycle after finance completes review.

## 8. Examples

- A EUR 18 taxi expense does not require a receipt under the general receipt threshold, but the business purpose must still be recorded.
- A EUR 28 meal requires a receipt.
- A hotel claim should be checked against HR-TRV-0173, not against the meal limits in this document.
