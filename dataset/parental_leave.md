# FakeCompanyABC Parental Leave and Return-to-Work Policy

## 1. Scope

This document describes FakeCompanyABC's internal notification and return-to-work procedures related to parental leave.

It does **not** replace statutory parental-leave rights. Where local law provides a greater entitlement or different requirement, local law takes precedence.

## 2. Notification

Employees should notify HR and their manager as early as reasonably possible when planning parental leave.

Where a specific statutory notice period applies, the statutory requirement must be followed.

## 3. Company-paid transition days

In addition to any statutory entitlement, eligible full-time employees who have completed at least **12 months of continuous service** may receive up to **5 company-paid transition days** associated with the birth or adoption of a child.

These transition days are separate from annual leave.

Part-time employees receive transition days on a pro-rata basis.

## 4. Return-to-work arrangement

An employee returning from parental leave may request a temporary reduced-work schedule for up to **8 weeks**.

The request should normally be submitted at least **4 weeks before** the planned return date.

Approval depends on role requirements, local law, and agreement with HR and the employee's manager.

## 5. Remote work after parental leave

Returning from parental leave does not automatically create an additional remote-work allowance.

Any remote-work arrangement must follow **Remote Work Policy HR-RMT-0204** unless a specific temporary exception is approved.

## 6. Annual leave interaction

Company-paid transition days are not deducted from the employee's annual-leave balance.

Unused annual leave remains governed by **Annual Leave Policy HR-LEV-0101**, including its carry-forward rules.

## 7. Examples

- An eligible full-time employee with 18 months of service may receive up to 5 company-paid transition days.
- An employee with only 8 months of continuous service is not eligible for the company-paid transition days under this policy, although statutory rights may still apply.
- A returning employee requesting temporary remote work must also consider HR-RMT-0204.
