# FakeCompanyABC Remote Work Policy

## 1. Standard remote-work allowance

Remote-work allowances depend on contractual work location and team requirements.

### Germany

Employees contracted in Germany may normally work remotely for up to **3 days per week**, subject to manager approval and team scheduling.

Employees are normally expected to work from a FakeCompanyABC office at least **2 days per week**.

### Pakistan

Employees contracted in Pakistan may normally work remotely for up to **2 days per week**, subject to manager approval and team scheduling.

Employees are normally expected to work from a FakeCompanyABC office at least **3 days per week**.

## 2. Working from another country

Working remotely from a country other than the employee's contractual work location is **not automatically covered** by the normal weekly remote-work allowance.

Cross-border remote work requires prior written approval from **both HR and the employee's manager** because tax, immigration, payroll, information-security, and employment-law requirements may apply.

Manager approval alone is not sufficient for cross-border remote work.

## 3. Security requirements

Employees working remotely must:

- use the company-managed device for company work;
- connect through the approved VPN when required;
- follow FakeCompanyABC information-security rules;
- prevent unauthorized persons from accessing company information;
- report loss or theft of company devices immediately.

## 4. Temporary exceptions

A department director may approve a temporary exception to the weekly office-attendance requirement for up to **4 consecutive weeks**.

An exception to office attendance does **not** automatically authorize cross-border remote work.

## 5. Examples

- A Germany-based employee working from home in Germany 3 days in one week may be allowed if the manager approves.
- A Pakistan-based employee normally has a maximum of 2 remote days per week.
- A Germany-based employee who wants to work from Spain for one week needs HR and manager approval even if the employee has unused remote-work days.
