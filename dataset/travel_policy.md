# FakeCompanyABC Business Travel Policy

## 1. Approval

All business travel must be approved by the employee's manager **before booking**.

International travel additionally requires the cost center to be identified before booking.

## 2. Air travel

For flights with a scheduled duration of **6 hours or less**, employees should book economy class.

For flights with a scheduled duration of **more than 6 hours**, premium economy may be booked with manager approval.

Business class requires director-level approval and a documented business justification.

## 3. Hotel limits — current policy

The following nightly hotel limits apply from **1 January 2026**:

| Location | Maximum reimbursable room rate per night |
|---|---:|
| Germany | EUR 160 |
| Belgium | EUR 170 |
| Spain | EUR 150 |
| Pakistan | PKR 32,000 |

The limit applies to the room rate including mandatory hotel taxes, but excluding separately charged breakfast where breakfast is reimbursable under the expense policy.

A higher rate may be reimbursed only if approved **before booking** by the employee's manager and the relevant cost-center owner.

## 4. Historical limits — do not use for travel from 2026 onward

Under **version 3.2**, effective from 1 January 2025 through 31 December 2025, the hotel limits were:

| Location | 2025 maximum room rate per night |
|---|---:|
| Germany | EUR 130 |
| Belgium | EUR 145 |
| Spain | EUR 125 |
| Pakistan | PKR 26,000 |

These values are retained here only for audit and historical-reference purposes.

## 5. Rail travel

Standard class should normally be used for rail journeys under **3 hours**.

First class may be used for rail journeys of **3 hours or more** if the fare is reasonable and manager approval has been obtained.

## 6. Booking channels

Employees should use the approved corporate travel provider when it is available for the destination.

Bookings made outside the approved channel must include a short justification when the expense claim is submitted.

## 7. Exact identifier note

This policy is identified as **HR-TRV-0173**. Questions referring to "TRV-0173", "HR-TRV-0173", or "travel policy 0173" refer to this policy.

## 8. Examples

- A hotel in Germany costing EUR 155 per night is within the 2026 standard limit.
- A Germany hotel costing EUR 145 per night was above the standard 2025 limit but is below the 2026 limit.
- A Spain hotel costing EUR 158 per night in 2026 requires advance approval because the standard limit is EUR 150.
